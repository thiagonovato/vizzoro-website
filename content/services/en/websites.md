## What's included

- A site built around what your customers actually came to do, not a generic template
- Fast loading on real connections and real phones, not just on a fast office wifi
- Accessible markup, so it works with screen readers and keyboard navigation
- Clean SEO fundamentals in place from day one: metadata, sitemaps, structured data

## Our process

We start from content and structure, then design, then build. That order matters: a beautiful layout built around placeholder text tends to fall apart once real copy goes in.

## Good fit for

Businesses that need a site people trust on the first look and can actually navigate on a phone.
